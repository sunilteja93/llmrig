# LLMRig Code of Conduct

LLMRig is an open-source project for people with different backgrounds, experience levels, and viewpoints. Participation should remain constructive and professional.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful when reviewing code, issues, benchmarks, and design ideas.
- Critique technical work, not the person who submitted it.
- Welcome newcomers and explain project conventions when practical.
- Share reproducible evidence when making technical claims.
- Respect privacy. Do not publish another person's private information, machine paths, credentials, or personal data.
- Accept that maintainers may reject a change for scope, safety, maintainability, or lack of verification even when the contribution was made in good faith.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, personal attacks, deliberate disruption, doxxing, or repeated bad-faith behavior are not acceptable in project spaces.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close discussions, reject contributions, or restrict participation when behavior materially harms the project or its community.

For serious conduct issues, use a private contact method available through the repository or GitHub rather than posting sensitive details publicly.
